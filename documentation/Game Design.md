Game Objective:
Win Conditions -
 
Survivors:
At least 1 survivors lives by the time limit

Survivors 2nd Condition:
All infected die due to obstacles by the time limit

Infected:
Infect all survivors before time limit is over

Infected 2nd Condition:
All survivors die before time limit is over

Definition of Constructs - 
List of Constructs:
Objects - obstacles (pitfalls), platforms

Characters - Players

Items - Powerups

Actions - 
Survivors
Default movement(sprinting), Parrying, Double Jumping

Infected
Default Sprinting (slightly slower than survivor), Single Jump (Jumps higher than Survivor's single jump)

Mechanics - 
Infecting - Infected have to run up to a survivor and get within their infection range
(very short range), once they're in range they can press a key/button to infect 
(similar to among us killing) it takes 0.5s to complete their dash to the survivor for
succesful infection, assuming missed parry

Parrying - Survivor presses their parry key/button from the infected,
if they get the button off within 0.5s, parry is successful, 
1 parry charge every 30s

Comeback mechanic - powerups: speed powerup, or adjusting action speed (sprinting
base speed can be increaseed by 1.5x faster for 10 seconds. 
We spawn Powerups in random spawn locations on a schedule. 

Rules - 
If you're infected try to infect the survivors before the time limit is over.
Watch out for any obstacles throughout the map.

Survivors try to run away and live until the time limit is over. Watch out for any 
obstacles/throughout the map. 

Spawn-camping measure - To ensure that the infected aren't able to instantly win the game
we ensure there spawns are set far apart, and fairly throughout our map(s)

Systems - 
Time Limit

Multiplayer Lobby System (1 Screen, click play/start go into a loading screen
shows X/4, ready up button, status display (waiting on players, loading game)
when 4/4 shows up, 4 players have hit start, on a first come first served basis (first 4
players to click start), loading game status displays, automatically loaded into game)

Player Input System (wasd by default)

Audio System (music)

Player Environment
1 map - Post Apocalyptic Urban City (on earth)

Set of Constraints : 
movement restrictions: Parrying (stuns the infected for 2 seconds)

UI/Form Factor: Main Menu, Waiting Lobby Scene, Buttons( Start, Info, Settings), Game HUD
